# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **fazalabbas2002@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
